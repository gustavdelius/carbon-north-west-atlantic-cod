## 1. Executive Summary of the Reconstruction

We thank the referee for an exceptionally rigorous, constructive, and forensically detailed review. We agree completely with the central verdict: the v1 manuscript conflated distinct Northwest Atlantic stock complexes, contradicted post-2024 empirical assessments for NAFO Divisions 2J3KL Northern cod, 2J+3KL capelin, and Subareas 3–4 mackerel, treated literal synthetic constants as observed survey targets, misreported `mizer` edition-1 equations and Jacobian dimensions (`826` vs. `727`), mixed Forward Euler time steps (`dt = 0.1` vs. `0.2`) across artifacts, over-interpreted a straight-chord bisection midpoint as a universal cod-SSB separatrix, and claimed selective/adaptive management properties that had not been tested.

Rather than attempting a cosmetic Discussion edit, we executed all eight steps of the referee's **Concrete Revision Sequence** (`paper/referee_report.md:228–238`):

1. **Archived v1 intact** (`archive/v1_reviewed/` + `SHA256SUMS.txt`) and **reconstructed the paper from the ground up as a theoretical multispecies size-spectrum study** in relative simulation years ($t \in [0, 61]\text{ yr}$ for collapse and closure; $\tau = t - 61 \in [0, 40]\text{ yr}$ for policy experiments, extended to $\tau = 340\text{ yr}$ to verify asymptotic attractors).
2. **Built a complete parameter provenance and claim-to-source ledger** (`paper/provenance_ledger.md`) and repaired every bibliographic record (`paper/references.bib`), removing the unverified `delius2022mizer` entry and adding verified primary DFO assessments (`dfo2024reopen`, `dfo2025cod`, `dfo2025capelin`, `dfo2026mackerel`), Smith Sound studies (`rose2003monitoring`, `rose2011isolation`), mathematical stability analysis (`datta2011stability`), and the pinned `mizer` package citation (`mizer2026package`).
3. **Unified the entire computational pipeline (`R/00`–`R/04`)** on a single second-order solver (`method = "tr_bdf2"`, `dt = 0.05 yr`), refined all equilibria to `residual_tol = 1e-8`, programmatically audited every equation/exponent/grid dimension (`models/methods_audit.json`), and recorded a 43-artifact SHA-256 manifest (`models/reproducibility_manifest.json`) with execution logs (`models/logs/*.log`).
4. **Replaced universal scalar SSB thresholds** with documented straight-chord transition brackets (`[28.42, 28.48] kt` at $E_{\mathrm{cod}} = 0$; `[242.49, 242.54] kt` at $E_{\mathrm{cod}} = 0.15\text{ yr}^{-1}$), Newton-polished unstable saddle fixed points (`42.1 kt` and `321.7 kt`, each with $n_+ = 1$ positive eigenvalue out of `727`), equal/inverted-SSB counterexamples in contrasting prey spectra, fallback-free hysteresis continuation with `0.005 /yr` transition brackets (`[0.240, 0.245]` and `[0.150, 0.155] /yr`), eigenvalue tracking, direct escape-time curves, and $\tau = 340\text{ yr}$ asymptotic fate verification.
5. **Executed comprehensive numerical convergence and structural sensitivity sweeps** (`R/04_sensitivity_and_audit.R`), recording both regimes where bistability persists (including $R_{\mathrm{ext,Cod}} = 0$ with low-state extinction and invasion $R_0 = 0.822 < 1$, zero-invariant spawner-linked shelter `SaturatingShelterRDD`, `no_w = 150..200`, and recalibrated `van_leer` TVD transport) and parameter regions where bistability vanishes ($R_{\mathrm{ext}} \ge 1.5\%$, juvenile shelter export $w_{\mathrm{entry}} \ge 0.5\text{ g}$, $11/16$ cells of the $(r_{\mathrm{prey}}, s_\theta)$ phase diagram, symmetric larval overlap, $1.15\times \sigma_i$, $r_{\mathrm{Cod}} = 0.55$, and $r_{pp} = 20$).
6. **Implemented matched 6-gear policy experiments** separating `Capelin_Gear` from `Clupeid_Mackerel`, isolating tapering (`#6` vs. `#7`), testing Phase-I juvenile and adult cod bycatch (`E_byc` sweeps), testing a 1-year-lagged state-dependent feedback HCR under lognormal survey error (`CV = 0.25`, `16/16` recovery), and reporting multi-objective trade-offs across cumulative, transient, and asymptotic yields with zero monetary claims.
7. **Rebuilt all seven figures (`fig1`–`fig7`), seven manuscript tables (`Tables 1`–`7`), `paper/manuscript.pdf`, `README.md`, `.claude/skills/*/NOTES.md`, and the discrete-grid educational explorer (`docs/`)** from the single canonical result set.
