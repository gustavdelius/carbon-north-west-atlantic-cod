# Scope, Parameter Provenance, and Claim-to-Source Ledger

This document establishes the scientific scope, parameter and target provenance, and claim-to-source verification for the revised manuscript (*"Cultivation and depensation in a six-species size-spectrum model with external cod recruitment"*), addressing Referee Comments 1, 2, and 13.

---

## 1. Scientific Scope Declaration (Referee Comment 1)

- **Scope chosen:** **Theoretical multispecies size-spectrum modelling study** inspired by boreal–temperate Northwest Atlantic gadoid–forage shelf communities, **not** an empirical stock reconstruction or operational fishery management advisory for any specific Northwest Atlantic Fisheries Organization (NAFO) management unit.
- **Temporal convention:** All simulations use **relative model time** $t$ (in years), with $t \in [0, 61]$ for the illustrative collapse-and-moratorium trajectory ($E_{\mathrm{cod}}$ elevated during $t \in [3, 27)$, closed at $E_{\mathrm{cod}} = 0$ during $t \in [27, 61]$) and $\tau = t - 61 \in [0, 40]$ (extended to $\tau = 300\text{--}1000\text{ yr}$ for asymptotic fate verification) for post-collapse intervention scenarios. Calendar years (`1965–2066`) have been removed from all model trajectories, tables, figures, and interactive explorer axes.
- **Role of empirical Northwest Atlantic stock evidence:** Empirical stocks are cited solely as contrasting motivating and comparative cases demonstrating that real Northwest Atlantic cod ecosystems have **diverged** rather than followed a single post-1992 trajectory, with explicit NAFO stock boundaries, dates, metrics, and primary sources.

---

## 2. Parameter and Calibration Target Provenance Ledger (Referee Comment 2)

Every model input, allometric exponent, structural parameter, calibration target, and calibrated quantity in `R/01_build_and_calibrate_model.R` is classified below into one of five provenance categories:
1. **`mizer` allometric default (`edition = 1`)** — set automatically by `mizer::newMultispeciesParams()` in `mizer` `3.4.0.9003` (`defaults_edition = 1`).
2. **Literature-informed life-history scale** — order-of-magnitude trait scale motivated by published Northwest Atlantic gadoid, clupeid, scombrid, cottid, and pleuronectid biology, rounded to clean theoretical guild values rather than estimated from a single survey dataset.
3. **Illustrative / synthetic reference target** — chosen to define a coherent six-guild continental-shelf community in which large gadoids co-occur with pelagic planktivores, a benthic mesopredator, and a flatfish competitor; **not** derived from or validated against an empirical survey time series.
4. **Structural mechanism assumption** — imposed ecological interaction, density-dependence, or open-population assumption whose necessity is explicitly tested via structural sensitivity sweeps in `R/04_sensitivity_and_audit.R`.
5. **Internally calibrated model parameter** — solved numerically during steady-state calibration at the reference fishing effort vector $E_{\mathrm{cal}} = (0.15, 0.05, 0.15, 0.22)\text{ yr}^{-1}$ so that stationary model abundances, somatic growth rates, and reproduction levels are mutually consistent.

- **Note on external mortality $\mu_{\mathrm{ext},i}(w)$:** Because `z0` is explicitly supplied in `sp` and `z_ext` is omitted (`0`), `mizer` assigns size-independent background mortality $\mu_{\mathrm{ext},i}(w) = z_{0,i}$ across all consumer size bins (representing unmodelled seabird, marine mammal, disease, and senescence mortality).
- **Note on internally calibrated parameters (`h`, `gamma`, `ks`, `erepro`, `R_max`):** Iterating `matchGrowth()`, `matchBiomasses()`, `steadySingleSpecies()`, and `tuneSteadyState()` at $E_{\mathrm{cal}} = (0.15, 0.05, 0.15, 0.22)\text{ yr}^{-1}$ adjusts maximum intake coefficient $h_i$ (and linked $\gamma_i, k_{s,i}$) to match the von Bertalanffy $k_{\mathrm{vb},i}$ growth scale, scales abundance to match the synthetic reference targets $B_i^{\mathrm{ref}}$ at $E_{\mathrm{cal}}$ prior to switching on Beverton–Holt regulation, and computes `erepro` and `R_max` from the imposed reproduction levels $r_i$. Note that $E_{\mathrm{cal}}$ ($E_{\mathrm{cod}} = 0.22\text{ yr}^{-1}$) was deliberately chosen near the upper exploited branch so that the reference fished state $E_{\mathrm{hist}}$ ($E_{\mathrm{cod}} = 0.15\text{ yr}^{-1}$) sits comfortably inside the high-cod basin of attraction.
