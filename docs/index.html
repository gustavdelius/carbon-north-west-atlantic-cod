1. Theoretical Mechanism & Empirical Scope

Ontogenetic Role Reversal in a Synthetic Boreal-Shelf Food Web

This model investigates a conditional ecological mechanism—not an empirical reconstruction of Northwest Atlantic stock history or operational management advice. All simulation axes use relative simulation time (τ = 0 marks the end of a 34-year directed-cod closure following a 27-year overfishing ramp).

Upper Attractor (Ecod = 0)

Cultivated High-Cod State

Abundant adult cod (1018.2 kt SSB; baseline non-cod fleets active) crop planktivorous pelagics and benthic sculpins, keeping cumulative early-life hazard H(15 g) = ∫(μ/g) dw at 0.19 (83.1% survival to 15 g; net reproductive ratio R0 = 1.21).

Lower Attractor (Ecod = 0)

Depensatory Forage-Dominated State

After prolonged cod depletion, released mackerel, herring, and sculpin inflate early-life cod hazard to H(15 g) = 2.82 (88.7% direct predation, 0.9% slower growth alone, 10.4% predation × growth synergy), cutting R0 to 0.88 despite elevated adult cod feeding.

Empirical Context & Structural Limits

Where the Mechanism Holds—and Fails

Bistability disappears under weaker larval overlap (sθ ≤ 0.75 at rprey ≥ 0.70, and for all tested rprey at sθ ≤ 0.50), symmetric fish-larval overlap, wider feeding kernels (1.15×σ), stronger cod compensation (rCod = 0.55), fast resource turnover (rpp = 20), or exogenous cod recruitment ≥ 1.5%. Real Northwest Atlantic stocks differ sharply (e.g. NAFO 2J3KL Northern cod rebuilt to ~524 kt by 2025 alongside depleted capelin).

4. Hysteresis & Settled Transition Brackets

Equilibrium Continuation Scans Across Cod and Pelagic Effort

Local linearization over all 727 active consumer + resource coordinates confirms negative leading real parts at both coexisting Ecod = 0 equilibria, separated by an unstable saddle fixed point (42.1 kt Cod SSB along the saddle manifold; note that equal-SSB states with different prey spectra have opposite fates). Shaded bands show refined 0.005 /yr settled transition intervals.

A. Hysteresis vs. Directed Cod Effort (Ecod)

Forward continuation from the cultivated equilibrium transitions between 0.240 and 0.245 /yr as the leading real eigenvalue approaches zero; backward continuation remains on the lower branch down to Ecod = 0.

B. Escape Hysteresis vs. Pelagic Effort (Epel at Ecod = 0)

Settled continuation from the low-cod state escapes between 0.150 and 0.155 /yr over multi-century horizons, whereas escaping within a 20–40 yr management window requires much stronger predator thinning.

5. Matched 6-Gear Policy Experiments & Multi-Objective Trade-Offs

Separating Tapering, Capelin Sparing, Bycatch Fragility, and Aggregate Yield

In our 6-gear extension (separating Capelin_Gear from Clupeid_Mackerel and adding Cod_Bycatch), rebuilding cod reduces equilibrium aggregate tonnage relative to harvesting hyperabundant forage stocks, while capelin-sparing (keeping Ecap = 0.15) accelerates cod recovery because herring, mackerel, and sculpin—not capelin—dominate early-life cod hazard.
