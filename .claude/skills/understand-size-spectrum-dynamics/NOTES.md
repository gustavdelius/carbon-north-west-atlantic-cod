# Project Notes — `understand-size-spectrum-dynamics`

- **Exact allometric & rate settings under `defaults_edition(1)` (`mizer 3.4.0.9003`):**
  - Intake exponent `n = 2/3`, metabolic exponent `p = 0.7`, search-volume exponent `q = n + lambda - 2 = 0.7466667` (`lambda = 2.08`), `w_max = 1.5 * w_inf` (`22.5 kg` for Cod), size-independent external mortality `ext_mort = z0` (`0.20 /yr` for Cod), and power-law semichemostat resource turnover `r_R(w) = 10 * w^(-1/3) /yr` below `w_pp_cutoff = 10 g`.
- **Predator-resolved hazard & opposing ontogenetic feeding shifts (`E_cod = 0` equilibria):**
  - Cumulative early-life hazard `H = integral (mu / g) dw` across `[0.001, 15) g` rises from `0.19` in the cultivated state to `2.82` in the depensatory state (`Delta H = +2.64`: `88.7%` direct mortality increase, `0.9%` growth slowdown alone, `10.4%` mortality x slower-growth synergy), reducing stationary cohort survival 14.0-fold (`83.1%` to `6.0%`).
  - Mackerel (`H = 1.15`), Herring (`0.98`), and Sculpin (`0.42`) account for `93.8%` of predation hazard on `< 15 g` Cod in the depensatory state (`2.72` total predation hazard + `0.10` external), while Capelin contributes only `0.16` (`5.9%` of predation hazard). Across all pre-maturation sizes (`< 850 g`), `79.4%` of predation hazard occurs below `15 g` and `92.6%` below `50 g`. Consequently, sparing Capelin (`E_cap = 0.15`) while harvesting Herring/Mackerel (`0.65`) and Sculpin (`0.45`) accelerates Cod rebuilding rather than hindering it.
  - Cod feeding level `f(w)` in the depensatory state is **1.35%–11.68% lower** over `0.05–5 g` (`0.07%–15.00%` lower over `0.002–15 g`) due to shared resource grazing, **yet higher above `200 g`** (`+52%` to `+321%` for `1–10 kg` adults) due to abundant forage prey. Demographic `R0(mu, g, er)` drops from `1.21` (cultivated) to `0.027` under juvenile `mu_low, g_low` with `er_high`, and reaches `0.88 < 1` even after accounting for elevated adult reproductive output `er_low`.
- **Canonical solver & structural boundaries (`R/04_sensitivity_and_audit.R`):**
  - All simulations share `method = "tr_bdf2"`, `dt = 0.05` (converging the 20-yr `joint_taper_f15` policy to `433.6 kt` at `tau = 40 yr` and `776.3 kt` asymptotically, resolving the v1 `dt = 0.1` vs `0.2` Forward-Euler discrepancy).
  - Bistability requires strong asymmetric early-life predation overlap and relatively weak cod recruitment compensation: it disappears (monostable high-cod recovery) when `s_theta <= 0.75` for `r_prey >= 0.70` (and for all tested `r_prey` when `s_theta <= 0.50`), under symmetric fish-larval overlap, wider feeding kernels (`1.15x sigma`), stronger cod compensation (`r_cod = 0.55`), fast resource regeneration (`r_pp = 20`), `R_ext / R_cal >= 1.5%`, or on coarse `no_w = 80` / un-recalibrated `van_leer` grids.
