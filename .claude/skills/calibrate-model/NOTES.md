# Project Notes — `calibrate-model`

- **Theoretical scope & synthetic targets (`R/01_build_and_calibrate_model.R`):** This repository implements a theoretical six-species boreal-shelf size-spectrum model (`Capelin`, `Herring`, `Mackerel`, `Sculpin`, `Plaice`, `Cod`). The `biomass_observed` column supplies synthetic illustrative cutoff-biomass targets (`520, 380, 190, 140, 260, 680 kt` above `2, 10, 15, 15, 20, 50 g`) required by `mizer::matchBiomasses()`, not empirical survey series from a single stock boundary.
- **Calibration effort design choice:** Calibration is performed at `EFFORT_CAL` (`Cod_Trawl = 0.22 /yr`, `Pelagic = 0.15 /yr`, `Mesopredator = 0.05 /yr`, `Flatfish = 0.15 /yr`) so that the baseline sustainable reference fishery (`EFFORT_HIST`, `Cod_Trawl = 0.15 /yr`) lies comfortably inside the upper cultivated basin below the `[0.240, 0.245] /yr` transition interval.
- **Pre- vs. post-subsidy residuals at `EFFORT_CAL`:** Before adding exogenous cod recruitment (`r_refuge`), alternating `matchGrowth()`, `matchBiomasses()`, `steadySingleSpecies(..., keep = "biomass")`, `setBevertonHolt()`, and `tuneSteadyState()` matches the synthetic cutoff-biomass targets to `< 1e-12%` with physiologically consistent `erepro` (`2.12e-6` for `Cod` to `1.70e-3` for `Capelin`). Adding `r_refuge = 0.011 * RDD_cal["Cod"]` (`2.290e6 /yr`) and settling at `EFFORT_CAL` shifts Cod cutoff biomass by `+1.06%` (`687.2 kt` vs. `680 kt`) and prey cutoff biomasses by `-1.1%` to `-3.4%`.
- **Saved `MizerParams` objects (`saveParams()`, `residual_tol = 1e-8`, `attractor = "fixed_point"`):**
  - `models/cod_f15_params.rds` — Baseline sustainable fishery (`E_cod = 0.15 /yr`, Cod SSB `776.3 kt`, biomass residual `5.93e-9 /yr`).
  - `models/cod_high_f0_params.rds` — Zero-cod-fishing cultivated equilibrium (`E_cod = 0.00 /yr`, baseline non-cod fleets active; Cod SSB `1018.2 kt`, biomass residual `1.49e-9 /yr`). Note: 4 ultra-trace Cod tail bins above `9.5 kg` (`N ~ 1e-8..1e-5`, `< 0.04 g` total) have `g(w) = 0` and therefore `per_capita` residual `-z0 = -0.20 /yr` while biomass drift is `< 4e-11`.
  - `models/cod_low_f0_params.rds` — Zero-cod-fishing depensatory equilibrium (`E_cod = 0.00 /yr`, identical biological/interaction/resource/gear parameters to `cod_high_f0_params.rds`; Cod SSB `15.8 kt`, biomass residual `2.28e-9 /yr`; exogenous input represents `13.5%` of total Cod `RDD` in this low state).
  - `models/cod_low_f15_params.rds` — Low-cod equilibrium at `E_cod = 0.15 /yr` (`Cod SSB = 1.3 kt`, biomass residual `1.81e-9 /yr`).
  - `models/cod_t61_params.rds` — Transient post-closure state at relative year `t = 61` (`Cod SSB = 9.17 kt`) integrated with `method = "tr_bdf2"` and `dt = 0.05`.
