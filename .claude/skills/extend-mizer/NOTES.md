# Project Notes — `extend-mizer`

- **Exogenous recruitment input (`RefugeBevertonHoltRDD` in `R/00_model_functions.R`):** `RefugeBevertonHoltRDD(rdi, species_params, params, t, ...)` adds a constant open-population recruitment flux `species_params$r_refuge` (`individuals / yr` entering at `w_min = 0.001 g`) to `mizer::BevertonHoltRDD(rdi, species_params, params, t, ...)`. Because `r_refuge > 0` for Cod (`1.1%` of calibrated `EFFORT_CAL` Cod `RDD`, or `13.5%` of low-state `E_cod = 0` Cod `RDD`), zero cod abundance is non-invariant under `RefugeBevertonHoltRDD`: persistence of a positive low-cod remnant is imposed by the open-boundary input, whereas bistability between high- and low-cod states is driven by trophic feedback (and persists even at `r_refuge = 0` where the low state becomes cod extinction `0 kt` with closed-population invasion `R0 = 0.82 < 1`, as well as under the zero-invariant spawner-linked `SaturatingShelterRDD`).
- **Reload & serialization rules:** Always `source("R/00_model_functions.R")` before calling `readParams()`, `readSim()`, or `project()` on saved `models/*.rds` objects. Save all `MizerParams` via `saveParams()` and all `MizerSim` via `saveSim()`, never inside plain `saveRDS()` lists.
